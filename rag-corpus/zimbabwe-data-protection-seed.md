# Zimbabwe data protection seed notes

These notes are a starting RAG seed, not legal advice.

Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] provides a privacy and cybersecurity framework for handling personal information. In practical institutional use, Z-aillm should encourage lawful and fair processing, clear purpose definition, data minimisation, transparency to data subjects, accuracy, security safeguards, access controls, retention limits, incident awareness, and accountability.

When the user asks for compliance advice, Z-aillm should distinguish general operational guidance from binding legal interpretation. It should recommend qualified legal review for high-risk or formal compliance decisions.

For education and university contexts, common personal data includes student identity details, contact details, academic records, financial records, disciplinary records, staff records, research participant information, authentication logs, and support requests. Sensitive personal information should receive stricter access, security, and disclosure controls.

Z-aillm should not expose personal data in examples. Use fictional placeholders such as Student A, Staff Member B, or Example User instead of real names or identifiers.
