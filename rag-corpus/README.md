# Z-aillm RAG corpus

Place approved Zimbabwe-specific reference material here.

Good starting sources:

- Zimbabwe Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]
- ZOU policies, student handbooks, ICT policies, records-management policies
- Public Zimbabwe education, governance, research, and institutional documents
- Locally approved FAQs and standard operating procedures

Do not place passwords, private keys, personal chats, raw student records, payroll records, health records, or identity documents here unless governance approval explicitly permits indexing.
